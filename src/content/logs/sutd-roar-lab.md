boxes:
  - heading: What I built
    text: Secondary control systems on an STM32 microcontroller for Saber, the lab's four-legged all-terrain robot. I designed and implemented its failsafe mechanism, which is now being integrated into the robot. On the side, I benchmarked Open-RMF-type middleware across IoT and robotics into a reference database, and reviewed proposal documents for an AI Centre of Excellence initiative.
  - heading: Challenges
    text: The electrical side was new to me. My first task was a faulty motor, and I traced the circuitry down to the specific chip that had failed. Later, Saber broke down late one evening; with a teammate I found the affected motors, made the changes and had the robot ready for a full repair the next morning.
  - heading: What I learned
    text: How to pick up unfamiliar hardware quickly and debug to the root cause instead of the symptom. Also how a lab team works day to day, taking ownership of my own tasks while keeping everyone updated.
  - heading: Outcome
    text: My failsafe is being integrated into Saber, which won Gold at the Melbourne Design Awards 2026. I also showcased Saber to the public at Raikan Ilmu 2026 at Our Tampines Hub.
