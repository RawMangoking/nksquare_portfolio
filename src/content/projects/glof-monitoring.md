A team project for Smart India Hackathon 2024. Google Earth Engine scripts measure water and ice area on glaciers such as Chhota Shigri from Sentinel-2 imagery, and a dashboard shows the trends. The design pairs this with on-site sensors and an LSTM model to raise early warnings of glacial lake outburst floods.
